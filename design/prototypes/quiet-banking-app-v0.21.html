How portfolio growth will be measured

Aggregate the holdings’ dated investor cash flows at the selected-set reporting endpoint, carrying older saved values unchanged. Money put in is negative; money paid out is positive. Same-day equal outgoing and incoming amounts offset without transfer matching. Opening values are starting outflows and endpoint values are terminal inflows.

Same-holding reinvestment and retained account interest have no boundary cash flow; they are captured through units/account value. Do not record a second contribution for the same reinvestment.

Overall growth is cumulative over the tracking period, not annualised. Individual rows will use annualised returns when calculable. No division by the sum of recycled contributions. Gain and return recalculate from these synthetic records after each accepted change; no live market feed.
